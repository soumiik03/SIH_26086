# Step 11 Temporal Validation

- Train: 2020–2023, ending with a 37-day boundary purge.
- Calibration: first half of 2024.
- Calibration-method selection: second half of 2024, with a gap.
- Test: 2025, untouched during training, feature selection, calibration selection, and model selection; final test rows exclude the terminal 37-day label-completion window.
- 2026 is inference-only.

No random train/test split is used.
