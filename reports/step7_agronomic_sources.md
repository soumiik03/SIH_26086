# Step 7 agronomic sources

## Enabled profile: Aman rice

| Source | Use in Step 7 | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisories for Farmers 2025](https://icar.gov.in/sites/default/files/Circulars/ICAR-En-Kharif-Agro-Advisories-for-Farmers-2025.pdf) | West Bengal Aman timing | Seedbed preparation May 20–June 5 and transplanting June 10–June 25. |
| [ICAR-CRIDA contingency plan for North 24 Parganas](https://www.icar-crida.res.in/CP/West_Bengal/BCKKV,%20Kalyani/WestBengal%2013-North%2024%20Parganas-31-12-2011.pdf) | Contingency interpretation | Documents delayed transplanting through the first week of August, dry-spell life-saving irrigation/moisture management, and excess-rain drainage/waterlogging measures. |

The enabled profile uses May 20–August 7 as a conservative sourced decision window, with June 10–June 25 recorded as the preferred transplanting interval. This is not a claim that all locations or crop stages share one calendar; the advisory explicitly instructs local confirmation.

## Rule-relevant evidence matrix

| Crop | Sowing period | Rainfall / dry-spell evidence | Excess-rain sensitivity | Crop-stage / irrigation implication | Geography |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aman rice | Seedbed May 20-June 5; transplant June 10-June 25; contingency delay through first week of August | CRIDA contingency plan covers 15-20 day dry spells after sowing and life-saving irrigation/moisture management | Continuous high rainfall and waterlogging require excess-water drainage; postpone nitrogen top dressing | Nursery/transplanting timing matters; irrigation preparedness is conditional on deficit risk, never water excess | West Bengal profile; contingency evidence specifically North 24 Parganas |

The sources do not provide a universal numeric probability threshold. The implementation therefore uses named, disclosed boundaries inherited from the repository's existing risk/advisory framework and treats them as configurable decision-policy thresholds, not agronomic measurements.

## Regional candidate source

The [West Bengal Department of Agriculture diversified cropping programme](https://agriculture.wb.gov.in/diversified-cropping-program-for-dry-land-rainfed-condition) documents maize, groundnut, mustard, sesame, lentil, and arhar in specified dryland/rainfed districts. These crops are not enabled by this change because the current implementation lacks an equally reviewed rule profile for the supported forecast contract.

## Governance

Sources are recorded in the crop profile and this report. No unsupported crop, synthetic agronomic probability, weather feed, or new predictive model is introduced.
